Tvoli Support

Tvoli works with Samsung, LG, Google TV / Android TV, Roku, Vizio, Philips and Sony smart TVs over Wi-Fi.

Tvoli can't find my TV

  • Put your iPhone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks often block devices from seeing each other.
  • Allow Local Network: Settings › Apps › Tvoli › Local Network.
  • Turn the TV on. Some TVs stop answering in standby.
  • Turn off VPN on your iPhone.
  • Add the TV by IP address. You'll find it in the TV's network settings.

Pairing

  • Samsung, LG, Philips: accept the prompt on the TV screen.
  • Google TV / Android TV, Sony, Vizio: enter the code shown on the TV.
  • Roku: no pairing. Turn on Settings › System › Advanced › Control by mobile apps.
  • Sony: turn on Remote start and IP control in the TV's network settings.

The TV won't turn on from Tvoli

Many TVs switch off their network when fully off. Turn on the TV's "Wake on LAN", "Quick start" or "Network standby" setting.

Restore a purchase

Open Tvoli Pro and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
