Tvoli Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Tvoli is a TV remote for iPhone and iPad. It does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Data we collect

None. Tvoli has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We run no servers that receive information about you or your use of the app.

What stays on your device

  • The TVs you add: name, brand, local IP address and, where the TV needs it, a pairing token.
  • Scenes you create.

Tvoli stores these in your device's keychain, restricted to this device. They are not included in backups and never leave your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app removes them.

Local network

Tvoli talks directly to TVs on your own Wi-Fi network to find them and send commands. This traffic does not pass through us. Text you type in the keyboard screen goes only to your TV.

Purchases

Apple processes Tvoli Pro subscriptions and purchases. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Tvoli collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
